6. Strategic Answers to Your Unprompted RFQ Questions (Section 8a)

In Section 8a of your RFQ, you asked five specific, candid questions because you didn’t know the answers and suspected they mattered. Here is our direct, operator-first counsel:

Q1: Given the undecided items (Section 2.1), should we decide before scoping, or should you help us decide?

Our Answer: Do not delay scoping to resolve these internally. The right partner helps you make these decisions by presenting clear tradeoffs. For example, delaying hardware digital keys until lock vendors are standardized saves you tens of thousands of dollars right now, while building a mobile web check-in flow immediately delivers 80% of the guest benefit at 20% of the cost.

Q2: Is six properties with different seasonalities normal complexity or does it alter the build?

Our Answer: It fundamentally changes the database design compared to a single large hotel. In a single hotel, you have one rate calendar and one staff pool. Across six properties in two states, you need:

  • Independent rate plan rules (Moab's peak spring/fall vs. Breckenridge's ski winter).
  • Shared roaming staff permissions (e.g. your maintenance lead covering both Copperline and Wren House).
  • Centralized ownership reporting that normalizes metrics across properties with wildly divergent room counts (19 rooms vs 51 rooms).

We built this multi-tenant capability directly into the POC schema from day one.

Q3: How many people from your team need to be involved, and for how much of their time?

Our Answer: You do not need an army of project managers. Here is the realistic commitment:

  • Marcus (CEO): 1 hour per week for our Friday executive demo & sign-off.
  • Revenue Manager: 2 hours per week during Phase 1 & 2 to confirm rate structures and OTA mappings.
  • Property GMs: One 45-minute group feedback session during week 2 to review the front desk and housekeeping flows.

Q4: If we had to cut something to hit the ski-season deadline, what should be cut first?

Our Answer: Exactly what our roadmap reflects:

  1. Cut First: Physical BLE Digital Key hardware integration (avoid physical lock installations during peak ski season).
  2. Cut Second: Complex dynamic pricing algorithms (keep manual rate adjustments in the software while automating OTA distribution).
  3. Never Cut: Core multi-property room inventory, atomic overbooking protection, front-desk check-in, and billing settlement.

Q5: Is there anything in this RFQ that, if insisted upon as written, would be a mistake?

Our Answer: Yes — two things:

  1. Building a Native iOS/Android App: For a 6-property boutique group, requiring guests to download an App Store app has a 90%+ abandonment rate. A high-performance, warm Progressive Web App (PWA) accessed via SMS link delivers the exact same polished experience with zero friction.
  2. Direct Custom Coding to OTA APIs: Directly building and maintaining separate API connectors to Expedia, Booking.com, Airbnb, and Google Hotel Ads is an expensive trap that breaks whenever OTAs update their protocols. Connecting your inventory to a certified channel distribution API (like SiteMinder or Channex) gives you instant access to all channels with 99.9% uptime.

7. Architecture, Security & Data Ownership

7.1 Technology Stack Rationale

  • Frontend Client: React 18 + TypeScript + Vite. Ultra-fast, zero-bloat, responsive interface accessible on desktop front desk screens, mobile phones, and shared operational tablets.
  • Backend Engine: Node.js + Express + TypeScript. Structured with strict domain boundaries, Zod schema validation on all inputs, and transactional service layers.
  • Database: Relational SQL (SQLite for agile local testing → PostgreSQL on Supabase/AWS for cloud production). Provides ACID transactional guarantees essential for inventory management.

7.2 Absolute Data Sovereignty (Zero Vendor Lock-In)

Your previous PMS vendor trapped you with proprietary data formats. With LumenStay:

  • You Own 100% of the Code & Data: All database schemas, guest histories, and application code are transferred completely to Lumen Hospitality Group LLC.
  • Automated CSV & SQL Backups: Point-in-time recovery and automated daily snapshots hosted in your own cloud account (AWS or Supabase).
  • PCI Compliance Handled via Hosted Fields: Credit card numbers never touch LumenStay servers. We embed Square / Stripe hosted tokenization fields, keeping your business in the lightest PCI-DSS Self-Assessment Questionnaire (SAQ A) scope.

7.3 Offline Fallback & Friday Rush Protection

In Section 6b of your RFQ, you asked: "What happens if downtime occurs during a Friday check-in rush?"
LumenStay incorporates an Automated Daily Arrival Manifest. Every morning at 06:00, the system compiles and locally caches an encrypted arrival, stayover, and room status sheet. If an internet provider outage ever strikes Aspen or Breckenridge, your front desk can print or view the local offline manifest to check guests in manually without missing a beat.
